# Help & Support

## About this website

This platform is a tournament management website where users can create tournaments, join competitions, manage teams, and follow match results.

### What users can do

- Create and manage tournaments
- Browse tournaments by game and status
- Join tournaments as a participant
- Create or join a team
- View upcoming and completed matches
- Track standings and results
- Receive announcements for tournament updates
- View their profile and account activity

## Account and profile

- Users can sign up and log in with their email and account credentials.
- Users can update or choose a profile icon.
- Users can view their own profile and participation history.
- Admin users may manage tournament setup and progression.

## Tournament actions

- Tournament organizers can create a tournament with a name, game, format, entry fee, prize pool, and participant limits.
- Tournaments can have scheduled dates, locations, and descriptions.
- Admins can create matches, schedule them, and record results.
- The platform updates standings automatically based on match winners.
- Announcements can be posted to keep participants informed.

## Teams and participation

- Users can create teams and invite others to join.
- Team owners and members can be tracked in the platform.
- A team is usually associated with a specific game.
- Participating in tournaments may require joining a team or registering for the event.

## Matches and brackets

- Tournament matches can be created by admins.
- Each match includes two teams, a round name, and a schedule when available.
- Match results can be saved and used to update the tournament standings.
- Bracket progression depends on a supported tournament format and match outcomes.

## Notifications and support

- The platform provides announcements and status updates for ongoing tournament activity.
- Users can ask support questions in the help chat for product guidance.
- Support answers are based on the public website help information and safe product data.

## Security and privacy

- Account credentials are protected and handled securely.
- Sensitive account details and secret values are not available through the support chatbot.
- If you need to recover or change account access, use the official sign-in and reset flow on the website.
- Do not share private credentials or security codes in the chat.

## Common questions

### Registration and account access

#### How do I sign up?

Use the registration page to create an account with your email and a secure password. After signup, you can log in and start using the platform.

#### Do I need an account to browse tournaments?

You usually need an account to join tournaments, manage teams, or access personal profile features. Public browsing may still be available depending on the page and permissions.

#### Can I change my profile icon?

Yes. Users can select a profile icon from the available options in their profile settings.

#### What if I forget my account details?

Use the official reset or recovery flow available on the website. The help chatbot does not provide password or private credential values.

### Tournament creation and participation

#### How do I create a tournament?

Use the admin workflow on the website to create a tournament. Add the tournament name, game, format, dates, prize pool, entry fee, and max team limit, then save and publish it.

#### What information should I include when creating a tournament?

Typical fields include the tournament title, supported game, format, location, start and end date, description, entry fee, prize pool, and maximum teams allowed.

#### How do I join a tournament?

Open the tournament details page, check whether registration is open, and follow the on-page registration or team join flow.

#### Can I join a tournament as a team?

Yes. Many tournaments are designed for team-based participation. You can create or join a team and then register that team for the tournament.

#### How do I know if a tournament is open or closed?

The tournament list and tournament details page usually show the current status, such as scheduled, open, active, or completed.

### Team management

#### How do I create a team?

From the team area, create a team with a name that matches the game you want to compete in. After it is created, you can manage members and invitations.

#### Can I invite other players to my team?

Yes. Team owners can invite other users by email or through the invitation flow in the platform.

#### What happens when someone joins my team?

They become a team member and can participate in the relevant tournaments or team-related actions based on the platform permissions.

#### Can I leave or remove a teammate?

Team management depends on the platform workflow and permissions. Owner and member actions are generally managed from the team settings or member list.

### Match reporting and results

#### Who creates matches?

Admin users or authorized tournament managers can create and schedule tournament matches.

#### How are matches scheduled?

Matches are scheduled with a round name, opponent teams, and an optional date or time depending on the tournament setup.

#### How do I report a match result?

Admins can update the match result through the match management page by entering the final scores and selecting the winner when appropriate.

#### Why does the standings table update automatically?

The platform applies match results to the standings, so winners and points are recalculated based on the tournament rules and the recorded outcome.

#### What if a tournament format is bracket-based?

Bracket progression follows the supported tournament format. Match winners can move forward to later rounds when the system is configured for bracket progression.

### Admin operations

#### What can an admin do in the system?

Admins can manage tournaments, schedule matches, report results, post announcements, and oversee tournament progression.

#### Can admins create announcements?

Yes. Admins can post announcements for tournament updates so participants stay informed about schedule changes, results, or important reminders.

#### Can admins edit tournament details after publishing?

Yes, depending on the current workflow and permissions. Tournament details such as dates, format, description, and prize settings may be updated as needed.

#### How are award and prize details handled?

Prize pool and entry fee values are part of the tournament settings. The platform supports these fields in the tournament setup.

### Payments and notifications flow

#### Is payment handled inside the platform?

The project includes payment-related logic and webhook support, but payment operations should follow the configured payment integration for valid transaction handling.

#### What should I do if a payment fails?

Check the payment flow in the platform and verify the transaction status through the official checkout or payment process. Do not share payment or security details in the chatbot.

#### How are notifications sent?

The platform uses announcements and status updates to notify participants about tournament activity. This may include schedule changes, match updates, and general progress information.

#### Where do I see tournament updates?

Users can view announcements, tournament pages, and match/standings updates in the website interface.

#### Can the chatbot tell me payment or transaction details?

No. The support chatbot does not expose private payment data, transaction secrets, or account security information.

### General product questions

#### What are the main features of the website?

The platform supports tournament management, team creation, match scheduling, standings updates, announcements, and participant account management.

#### How can the chatbot help me?

It can explain common website tasks, tournament flows, team actions, match reporting, and admin operations using the help guide and safe public product information.

#### Can I ask about private security or account data?

No. The support chatbot will not reveal private account information, payment details, password data, or secret values.

#### Can I ask about database records or internal configuration?

No. The support system only provides public-safe guidance and general product information.

## Important note

This help document is meant for general user guidance and product support. It does not include private account details, payment secrets, or sensitive platform information.
